<Section label="The brief">

A self-initiated brief: a mail-in electronics repair workshop in Rotterdam for anyone who would rather fix a device than replace it.

Most repairs happen behind a counter. You hand over your phone, wait, pay and hope for the best. Shops and manufacturers repair out of sight, DIY guides hand you the screwdriver, and refurb marketplaces sell you a replacement instead.

> Open Bench takes the empty corner: a repair done for you that you can watch. A fixed price first, a padded box in the post, and a photo of every step on the bench.

</Section>

<Section label="What I designed">

- The name, the line "Fixed in the open." and the campaign line "See every screw."
- A symbol: an O whose top quarter lifts like the lid of a device on the bench, with a wordmark drawn on the same grid.
- A palette taken from inside a device, Circuit Green, Board Green, Bench White and Solder Black, and a type pairing of Archivo and IBM Plex Sans.
- Packaging, business cards, signage, a billboard and social posts.
- The product: a quote flow, a repair timeline with photos of your own device, and the website.

</Section>
