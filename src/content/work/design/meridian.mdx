<Section label="The brief">

A self-initiated brief: create a digital bank for professionals aged 25 to 40 whose money is spread across salary, savings, investments and cards. It needed the credibility of a traditional bank with the simplicity of modern fintech, and a product that gives people one clear picture instead of five apps.

</Section>

<Section label="What I designed">

- The brand strategy and identity: Sage and Explorer archetypes, the line "Financial intelligence for what's next", and a symbol made of two overlaid, rotated nautical stars.
- A palette of Meridian Dark, Amber and Ivory, with a type system that switches between the brand fonts and free stand-ins through Figma variables.
- A 13-screen iOS prototype: welcome, sign-in, Today, insights, accounts, transactions, goals and a full send-money flow.
- Desktop and mobile landing pages and the app icon.
- A campaign and brand touchpoints: billboard, posters, social posts, stationery and merchandise.

</Section>

## Research

I mapped the category by tone and by colour. Banks sit in traditional and serious, challengers in modern and playful, and almost everyone owns blue, green or red. Modern and serious was empty, and warm amber was unclaimed.

## Strategy

The concept is direction through understanding: help people see where they are and what comes next. The Sage leads, guided by the Explorer, for people like Alex, a product manager whose money is fine but spread across five apps.

## Identity

A meridian is the line navigators use to know exactly where they are. That became the symbol, two overlaid nautical stars, and the main interface element: an amber route from today's balance to each goal, ending at the star. Amber is used sparingly so it always means "this is you".
