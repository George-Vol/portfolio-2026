<Section label="The problem">

Most people's money is spread across a bank app, a savings app, a brokerage and a card, so nobody has one clear picture. Meridian is a fictional digital bank I created to fix that: one view of everything, with plain-language insights about what changed and what you could do next.

</Section>

## What I built

A marketing site and a working preview of the logged-in app in Next.js, React and TypeScript. It runs entirely in the browser on sample data, so there's no backend to set up.

<Grid cols={2}>
<Callout label="Five app areas">

Today, Accounts with search, filters, recategorising and split payments, Goals with a monthly plan that updates the finish date, Pay with a keypad and review step, and Insights.

</Callout>
<Callout label="Undo everything">

A client-side store where every action can be undone, with tests for the state and goal-date logic using Node's built-in test runner.

</Callout>
<Callout label="Accessible components">

A component kit with no UI libraries, including a custom Select that follows the WAI-ARIA combobox pattern, and hover, focus, disabled and loading states on every control.

</Callout>
<Callout label="Ready to share">

Sign-up with inline validation, a one-click demo login, and full SEO: metadata, Open Graph images, sitemap and a web manifest.

</Callout>
</Grid>

## Designed first, then built

I designed the product in Figma before building it, so the code follows one system. The brand idea is direction through understanding, and the home screen turns it into a route: a line from today's balance to each savings goal, ending at the star from the logo. Colours, type, radii and motion all come from the brand guidelines as Tailwind tokens.
